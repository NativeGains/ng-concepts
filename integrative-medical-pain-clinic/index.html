Treatments

Pain treatments in Chicago and Rockford.

Not sure which is right for you? Call us

Prolotherapy

A non-surgical regenerative injection that starts a healing response in injured tendons, ligaments and other soft tissue. Used for rotator cuff tears, tennis elbow, knee, foot and hip pain, and certain types of back pain.

PRP and PRF injections

Platelet-rich plasma and platelet-rich fibrin to promote healing in injured joints, muscles, ligaments and tendons, including shoulder, knee and hip pain.

Trigger point injections and nerve blocks

Focused treatment for tender, painful areas within muscle that limit movement and flexibility.

Acupuncture

A complementary therapy for pain management, muscle relaxation and stress, as part of a comprehensive care plan.

Ketamine infusion therapy

For chronic pain and depression when traditional treatments haven't provided relief.

Knee arthritis injections

Sodium hyaluronic acid injections that lubricate and cushion the knee joint, a non-surgical option for joint pain.

Physical therapy

A one-on-one evaluation and an individualized program for musculoskeletal injuries and conditions.

About Linda Li, MD

Expert in pain management.

Dr. Linda Li trained at the University of Chicago rehabilitation program, affiliated with Schwab Rehabilitation Hospital, and is board-certified in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation.

She founded the Pain Management & Rehabilitation Center in Rockford in 2004 and opened her Chicago location in 2007. In 2017 the practice became Integrative Medical Pain Clinic, taking a functional approach to pain conditions.

She has special training in functional medicine, integrative medicine and environmental medicine, and focuses on the roots of chronic inflammation and the causes of pain.
